# Media provenance

All job photos are actual public Lavish Tree Service photographs. No generated job imagery, promotional composites, or third-party stock work is used. The owner requested using this business's Facebook work for the redesign. Original photographs and the logo remain the property of their respective owner.

## Facebook originals, retrieved October 24, 2026

The public page at https://www.facebook.com/lavishtreeservice/ identifies Lavish, links https://lavishtreeservice.com, and displays the matching phone 440-452-7240 and sales@lavishtreeservice.com. Its 24-hour emergency claim matches the existing website. Images were obtained from the public photo viewer without logging in.

- `facebook-crane-removal.webp` and its small variant: crane-assisted removal of a leafy tree section. Source: https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=844114395356145&set=pb.100092727774080.-2207520000 . Original 1638 × 2048, displayed March 4. Used in the opening hero.
- `facebook-bucket-crew.webp` and its small variant: crew member in an elevated bucket beside a tree. Source: https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=860481443719440&set=pb.100092727774080.-2207520000 . Original 1638 × 2048, displayed March 25. Used in the work gallery.

Dates are transcribed as displayed and are not represented on the website as a verified year. No suitable original landscape cutting video was available; the site uses a real still photo, not a simulated video. Facebook links open the canonical page directly rather than relying on login-gated embeds.

## Preserved original assets

Business logo and existing `rigging`, `fleet`, `green-truck`, `stump-grinding`, `storm-home`, and `summer-crew` photos came from https://lavishtreeservice.com in the original October 22 preview. These are preserved. The gallery uses the stump-grinding and storm-home originals alongside the newly sourced Facebook photo.

The Camyta mark and https://camytagroup.com footer attribution are retained.

## Fonts

DM Sans is preserved from the original preview. Barlow Condensed ExtraBold is added from the Google Fonts repository, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed (converted to WOFF without changing outlines). Both are self-hosted, with their SIL Open Font License text in `licenses/`. Existing unused Fraunces files are preserved for repository continuity but no longer used in the design.
